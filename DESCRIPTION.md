A complete bag replacement for WoW Classic Era — one combined, sortable, searchable window for your bags, bank, and keyring, rebuilt from the ground up for 2.0.

## Features
- Single combined bag window instead of juggling separate bag frames (a split-by-bag layout is one right-click away)
- Bank window with the same combined view, search, and sorting; keyring docked as a single quiet row
- **Find**: search every character you own from one window — one row per item with the total across all your characters and accounts; hover for the per-character breakdown
- **Fast sorting with slot locks**: right-click the sort button to mark slots that sorting must never touch
- **Categories**: rule-based item grouping you can toggle on per window, with your own custom rules
- Item quality glows, class-colored tooltips with per-character counts, and a money tooltip covering your whole roster
- **Cross-account gold and item counts** via Daseeki Nexus — other accounts' characters appear in tooltips and Find under "Other Accounts", kept current live while both accounts are on
- Follows your Daseeki Core theme and font throughout
- **Upgrading from Bags 1.x?** Everything carries over automatically on first login — characters, gold, settings, your grid layout, custom rules, keybinds, and sort locks. Your 1.x data is also preserved untouched for at least two releases, so rolling back is safe.

## Chat Commands
- `/dbg` — toggle the bag window
- `/dbg bank` — toggle the bank window
- `/dbg mesh` — cross-account sync status (this feature now lives in Daseeki Nexus; the command points you there)
- Settings live in the Daseeki hub (`/daseeki`)

## Requires
- **Daseeki Core** (required) — Bags 2.0 is built on the suite's shared UI foundation
- Optional: Daseeki Nexus — enables cross-account gold/item tracking
- Optional: Daseeki Raid Prep — adds its checklist toggle to the bag window's corner
